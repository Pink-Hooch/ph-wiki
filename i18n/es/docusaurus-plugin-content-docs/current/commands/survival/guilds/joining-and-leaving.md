# Joining and Leaving Guilds

Guilds may be open for anyone to join, or invite-only depending on their settings.

## Joining a guild

| Command                     | Description                                                                          |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `/g join <guild name>`      | Joins a guild or sends a request, depending on the guild's settings. |
| `/g unrequest <guild name>` | Removes your request to join that guild.                             |

## Leaving a guild

| Command    | Description                                |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| `/g leave` | Leaves your current guild. |

:::warning
Leaving a guild may remove your access to guild homes, guild claims, and guild chat.
:::
